0. WHAT WOULD MAKE THIS SPECIFICATION WRONG
================================================================================

Four defects have now been found by the next reader rather than by the analyst.
Expect a fifth. This section exists to make it cheaper to find.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
0.1  THE FOUR DEFECTS ALREADY FOUND, AND WHAT EACH LOOKED LIKE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

D1  THE JAR WAS SILENTLY ACTING AS A GATE. Executed depth was capped by free
    slots, so a 9-signal event squeezed into 4 was recorded as d4 and got the
    d4 gate instead of the d5+ gate. Every re-tiering is DOWNWARD, so the gate
    applied was always STRICTER than the market state warranted. 446 of 2,019
    admitted bars re-tiered, carrying a 1.66% loss rate against 3.95%. The book
    looked ~35 loss events safer than it was.
    SHAPE: a number better than its own mechanism could explain.

D2  THE DEPTH FLOOR WAS A POST-HOC FILTER, NOT AN ADMISSION RULE. 9,356 trades
    were opened and then deleted, 2,068 of them belonging to events valid in
    market terms. In a simulator they vanish; live they are open.
    SHAPE: a rule no engine could execute at decision time.

D3  A MECHANISM WAS INVENTED TO EXPLAIN A CORRECT NUMBER. Four non-SL losses
    were explained as a break-even lock netting negative against spread. The
    smallest possible lock is 12 points against a 3-point spread, so it cannot
    arise. The number was right; the explanation was fabricated.
    SHAPE: a plausible story attached to a figure nobody had traced.

D4  AN INSTRUCTION NO SECOND PARTY COULD EXECUTE. The gate masks were handed
    over as "built via the swept-spec mechanism, not the default p80/p20". That
    named nothing callable. The Developer fell back to ad[(var,'hi')] — the p80
    series — and a > p90 gate evaluated against p80 is a looser gate. Result:
    6,753 trades against 5,776 and 100 loss events against 42, all in the
    direction of gates not biting. Closed by shipping swept_thresholds.py.
    SHAPE: a handover that reads as complete and is not.
    SAME CLASS AS: the config sidecar that silently took the sacred path when
    its filename convention did not match, and the _adm_pass lookup that
    returned True on a missing tier-gate entry and opened LONG d1/d2 ungated.
    ALL THREE ARE PERMISSIVE FALLBACKS. A missing thing defaulted to permitted.

2. THE RULE, IN EXECUTION ORDER
================================================================================

STEP 1 — BAR ELIGIBILITY
  bar >= 6900          Dots_InitBars. Ring saturates at 5,204, so the floor
                       binds. First scannable bar 2026.01.26 20:54.
  ADX_Value >= 15
  Volume > 50
  Volume != 0
  NOT (EST_DayOfWeek == 5 AND (EST_Hour > 16 OR
       (EST_Hour == 16 AND EST_Minute >= 45)))

STEP 2 — SIGNAL QUALIFICATION

  297 signals. ALL 297 ARE F0 TRIPLE-CONVERGENCE: three conditions ANDed, each
  of the form VARIABLE:hi, VARIABLE:lo or VARIABLE:==N, plus the mandatory
  fourth term D2D_Trend_Dir == trade direction. ONE GRAMMAR, NO EXCEPTIONS, NO
  FAMILY SPLIT, NO SECOND EVALUATOR, NO ANCHOR.

  hi/lo are mechanism-D adaptive percentiles from dots_thresholds.py (rolling
  2,500 eligible bars, day-refreshed, floor-index), hi = p80 and lo = p20; or
  the structural constants (VWAP_Z +/-2; OR_Position 0.80/0.20, gated on a
  valid OR set).

  THE LISTING IS MECHANICALLY CHECKABLE. Every row is exactly three
  ' + '-separated conditions. A build MUST assert this and abort on any row
  that is not — the Developer's _assert_book_grammar does so. Verified at
  assembly of this document: 297 rows, 0 rows containing '->', 0 rows not of
  three-condition form, trigger F0 on all 297, 191 LONG / 106 SHORT.

STEP 3 — DEPTH. THIS IS NOT v1's DEFINITION.

  DEPTH IS THE NUMBER OF SIGNALS ADMITTED IN THE BATCH AT THAT BAR IN THAT
  DIRECTION. It is decided in STEP 4, before any position opens, and it is
  knowable at bar close.

  It is NOT the count of signals that qualify (a different, worse system).
  It is NOT v1's executed depth, which was only knowable AFTER a bar's
  admissions finished and so could not be evaluated at decision time.

STEP 4 — ADMISSION (FLOORED). THE BAR IS DECIDED AS ONE BATCH.

  At bar close, compute once:
      live = active trades that are NOT be_nudged
      free = MAX_POSITIONS - live                              (21 - live)

  Then for each direction d in (LONG, SHORT):
      A = signals of direction d qualifying at this bar AND not already
          holding a position
      if len(A) == 0            -> nothing this direction
      if free < 3               -> admit nothing this direction
      n = min(len(A), free)
      if n < 3                  -> admit nothing this direction
      evaluate STEP 5 and STEP 6 at depth n
      if they pass              -> ADMIT ALL n; free = free - n
      else                      -> admit nothing this direction

  NO POSITION IS EVER OPENED AND THEN REMOVED. There is no post-hoc filter.
  Backtest equals live, exactly.

  ADMISSION ORDER IS LONG THEN SHORT — and it is INERT. SHORT-then-LONG and a
  depth-first order both produce results IDENTICAL TO THE CENT (8.3).
  Implement either; it does not matter.

STEP 5 — GLOBAL GATE
  ATR_1M >= 20 at the entry bar. Raw ATR, not a percentile.
  Admits 36,526 of 177,251 bars = 20.61%.

STEP 6 — PER-TIER GATE STACK.  tier = min(depth, 5).

  tier   LONG                                            SHORT
  ----   ------------------------------------------      ------------------
  1      unreachable (floor is 3)                        unreachable
  2      unreachable (floor is 3)                        unreachable
  3      Micro_Hurst > p90                               Micro_Hurst > p90
  4      Micro_FailedBreak > p20 AND AT_Slope_ST > p90   FREE (measured, 5.3)
  5+     Micro_FailedBreak > p20                         FREE (measured, 5.3)

  All thresholds are mechanism-D adaptive percentiles at the entry bar, built
  by swept_thresholds.build_whole_dot_gates (3.4).
  A BUILD MUST NOT CARRY THE TIER-1 AND TIER-2 CELLS. They are unreachable
  under a floor of 3 and are not part of this system.
  ON THE TIER CAP min(depth, 5) AND WHAT IT MERGES: see 3.3. It is inherited
  and undocumented, and moving it was considered and declined.

STEP 7 — THE JAR
  live_lots = count of active trades that are NOT be_nudged. Cap 21.
  THE JAR COUNTS AT-RISK POSITIONS, NOT LOTS AND NOT OPEN POSITIONS. A trade
  past its break-even nudge cannot lose and frees its slot. This is why peak
  OPEN reaches 42 while peak AT-RISK is exactly 21.

STEP 8 — CONVICTION SIZING
  Multiplies lots. Never blocks. ASSIGNMENTS, not products, so max is 2.0:
      Micro_Hurst > p90 on longs                          x2.0
      D2D agreement (D2D_Signal == dir AND ADX >= 30
         AND Micro_Hurst >= p30), both directions         x2.0
      recentfb_sizing                                     DISABLED — see 3.2

STEP 9 — TRADE MANAGEMENT
  momentum      = Micro_LogReturn * direction >= 0.00012
  raw_risk      = ATR_1M * (4.0 if momentum else 2.0)
  initial_risk  = min(raw_risk, 150.0)      <- 150 POINTS. NOT DOLLARS.
  stop          = entry -/+ initial_risk
  base_risk     = min(ATR_1M * 2.0, 150.0)
  step_size     = 0.30 * base_risk
  be_trigger    = 1.00 * step_size
  be_lock_dist  = 1.00 * be_trigger
  lag           = 3 if momentum else 2
  SPREAD        = 3.0 points, charged on every trade
  Entry price is the bar CLOSE, so every trade opened on the same bar in the
  same direction has an IDENTICAL entry, stop, lag and conviction multiplier.

3.4  THE GATE MASKS — THE MODULE, THE FUNCTION, THE SEMANTICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  dots_thresholds.compute_adaptive_thresholds EMITS ONLY p80 ('hi') AND p20
  ('lo'), fixed in _D_SPEC at import. There is no public accessor for another
  percentile. USING ad[(var,'hi')] FOR A > p90 GATE IS A LOOSER GATE AND WAS
  DEFECT D4.

  CALL: swept_thresholds.build_whole_dot_gates(df)  ->  dict of bool arrays
        engine/swept_thresholds.py
  It does NOT reimplement the rolling walk. It temporarily substitutes
  dt._D_SPEC and calls the sacred function, restoring _D_SPEC in a finally, so
  the ring, eligibility mask, day-refresh and floor-index are BIT-IDENTICAL to
  production by construction.

  SEMANTICS, INHERITED FROM THE SACRED FUNCTION:
    ring            deque(maxlen=_ROLL_CAP), _ROLL_CAP = 2500
    ring admission  ADX_Value >= 15.0 AND Volume > 50.0. THE RING IS APPENDED
                    BEFORE THE DAY CHECK, so a refresh on bar i sees bar i's
                    own value if bar i is eligible.
    refresh         DAY-REFRESHED, NOT PER-BAR. Key is int(str(Time)[8:10]) —
                    THE DAY-OF-MONTH FIELD ONLY. Held flat between changes.
    percentile      FLOOR-INDEX, NOT INTERPOLATED.
                    idx = int(floor(count * pct)) clamped to [0, count-1];
                    value = sorted(ring)[idx]. Returns 0.0 when count < 2.
    warm-up         None. Before the ring fills the percentile is over
                    however many eligible bars exist. The caller owns the
                    bar >= 6900 floor.
    comparison      STRICT: > for high-side, < for low-side.

8.4  THE CEILING, AND THE ONE ASSUMPTION IT RESTS ON
  ceiling = MAX_POSITIONS x MAX_RISK x max conviction x lot
          = 21 x 150 x 2.0 x lot     ($1,890 at 0.30 = 37.8% of daily)

  It counts AT-RISK positions only. Peak OPEN reaches 42 while peak AT-RISK is
  exactly 21, and the difference is legitimate ONLY BECAUSE BREAK-EVEN-LOCKED
  POSITIONS CANNOT LOSE (6.3, from source and from measurement).

  IT IS TRUE OF THE SIMULATOR BY CONSTRUCTION. IT IS TRUE OF LIVE ONLY TO THE
  EXTENT FILLS ARE CLEAN. The engine fills at current_sl EXACTLY whenever the
  bar touches it. There is no slippage model and no gap model anywhere in the
  exit path. A gap through the lock fills BELOW it and a locked position would
  then lose. This frame cannot measure how far.
    - the ceiling is EXACT under the simulator's fill assumption
    - the ceiling is a LOWER BOUND live
    - every figure in 6.4 inherits the same assumption
    - the true live ceiling is (at-risk x MAX_RISK x conviction x lot) +
      (locked x gap-through-lock), and the second term is UNMEASURED
  THIS IS WHAT THE DEMO IS FOR.

8.5  THE SOLO/DUAL PATH WAS TESTED AND REJECTED
  A separately-gated d1/d2 admission path was built and swept — 10 free
  relocations at SHORT d1+d2, guarded so it fires only when the market offered
  1-2 signals and nothing was chopped.
  IT FAILS ON ITS OWN METRIC. Best exchange rate 0.28 episodes per loss event
  added, against 0.15 for simply enlarging the book — under 2x, one trial, no
  null. And the two genuinely strict gates (pass rates 0.489% and 0.684%)
  REACH ZERO NEW EPISODES while costing 2 and 13 loss events.
  STRICTNESS AND COVERAGE ARE ANTI-CORRELATED AND THE RANKING IS THE SAME
  ORDER. Any condition rare enough to be trusted alone will almost never be
  present on one of the two bars of a 2-bar episode. Arithmetic, not tuning.

B1  GAP FILLERS DO NOT SCALE WITH THE BASE LOT.
    portfolio_simulation_engine.py L302 hardcodes glots = 1.0; L306 reads the

    deployment at 0.30 would carry them at full size.
    DECIDE: scale them with the base lot, or disable them. DO NOT SHIP AS IS.

B2  THE ADMISSION BATCH MUST BE ONE PASS AT BAR CLOSE. Compute free once,
    decide the whole direction group, then open. An implementation that opens
    inside the loop and re-reads free reproduces defect D2.

B3  (RETIRED — the F1 anchor requirement no longer exists. See 0.3, closed.)

B4  NO POST-HOC FILTER ANYWHERE. If any stage deletes a trade after it was
    opened, the build is wrong.

B5  MACRO ORDERING. #define macros must precede first use. Standing check on
    any cross-section move in the EA.

B6  recentfb_sizing MUST BE FALSE. It is a live flag in conviction.py and the
    default is True. Shipping the default re-introduces a 20% deeper worst bar.

B7  ASSERT THE BOOK GRAMMAR AND ABORT ON VIOLATION. Every configured book row
    must be exactly three ' + '-separated conditions. A book that silently
    admits a different grammar is the class of defect this project has hit
    four times.

B8  THE GATE MASKS MUST COME FROM swept_thresholds.build_whole_dot_gates.
    Using ad[(var,'hi')] for a > p90 gate is defect D4 and it does not error —
    it produces 6,753 trades and 100 loss events that look plausible. Assert
    the pass rates in 3.4 before scoring.

B9  A MISSING CONFIG, MASK OR LOOKUP MUST RAISE, NEVER FALL THROUGH. Three
    separate defects in this project were permissive fallbacks. There is no
    correct silent default: scoring the wrong system with no error is strictly
    worse than refusing to start.

11. WHAT THIS DOCUMENT DOES NOT CLAIM
================================================================================

It does not claim this composition is optimal — 7 shows it is not
distinguishable from 59 alternatives out-of-sample.
It does not claim the composition has been tested forward. It has not.
It does not claim 42 loss events support a confidence interval on anything
day-keyed; those rest on 35.
It does not claim the intraday figures are engine output. They are a
reconstruction with a stated, arguable convention.
It does not claim an INDEPENDENT derivation has reproduced these numbers — a
second implementation reproduces them through the same book, engine fork and
gate module (0.3 R8).
It does not claim the six LONG orphans are justified by a statistic. They are
justified by a measured contribution and labelled as such in 4.2.
It does not claim min(depth, 5) has a derivation. It does not (3.3).
It does not claim June is understood. It is not (9.1).

WHAT IT DOES CLAIM: the signal layer was selected for 4-of-6-month consistency
and survived an independent rebuild at 84.6%; the one true holdout available is
the strongest month in the book; seven of seven months and twenty-six of
twenty-six ISO weeks are positive; and across sixty complete compositions and
three walk-forward windows every single one is profitable.

THE SIGNALS CARRY IT. THE COMPOSITION IS A PREFERENCE.
